About Us

At GameShop our greatest enthusiasm is for games. What started off as a passion project has developed into a venue where players not only make a purchase but also discover a sense of community. We carefully select each title, whether it's an independent game or a blockbuster, since it is important that great games should not only be sold but also discovered.

We ourselves have been gaming throughout our lives, and this is evident in all of our recommendations. No matter if you're picking up a controller for the first time or if you've been playing for decades, we're ready to assist you in finding games that are important to you.
